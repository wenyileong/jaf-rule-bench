JAF Rule Bench
A research tool accompanying the paper Jurisdictional Assignment Functions (SSRN 7351160).
It models how the rule deciding which state may tax a digital-asset holding affects how
much tax can be collected, and how often holdings end up untaxed or taxed twice.

Running it
1. Double-click JAF_Rule_Bench.exe. A small window opens (the engine) and the interface
   opens in your web browser. Everything runs on this computer; nothing is sent over the
   internet.
2. Windows may warn that the program is unrecognised (it is not code-signed): choose
   "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Press Quit in the interface, or close the engine window, to finish.
Results are saved in a "JAF Rule Bench" folder in your Documents.

In this folder
- JAF_Rule_Bench.exe: the program.
- templates: the coding template (blank, for real jurisdictions) and a completed example
  using invented states and synthetic data. The example's rows are not anyone's law and
  its values are not observations.
- docs: the user guide (start here), the model specification and the build plan.
- LICENSE.md, LICENSE-docs.txt: the licences (see below).
- THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt: licences of the components bundled in the program.
